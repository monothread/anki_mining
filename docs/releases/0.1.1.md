Small update: the audio of the cards moves to the back.

## What changed
- **Audio on the back of the card.** In the note type the app creates, the word and the sentence read aloud now play on the back, right below the reading (hiragana) and above the meaning. The front no longer plays any audio, so it does not give the answer away. The cards you already have keep their templates; to get the same layout in a note type you already use, remove `{{SentenceAudio}}` from its front and add it to its back after `{{Reading}}` (README, "Spoken word and sentence"). Changing templates makes Anki ask once for a full sync with AnkiWeb, so the app never does it for you.

## Install and update
Installed apps offer this version by themselves (a banner with **Install and restart**). Or download it: Windows `AnkiMining_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe` (not signed by Microsoft: SmartScreen may warn, choose *More info* > *Run anyway*), Linux `AnkiMining_0.1.1_amd64.AppImage`.
