The speech that reads words and sentences for the cards. Everything else in this folder is made by
`scripts/fetch-tts.sh` and is not stored in git:

- VOICEVOX CORE (MIT) and the VOICEVOX ONNX Runtime
- the Open JTalk dictionary
- the terms of the voice model and of the VOICEVOX ONNX Runtime, copied next to the files
- the voice model `0.vvm` (四国めたん, ずんだもん, 春日部つむぎ, 雨晴はう), under the VOICEVOX voice model terms
  (credit "VOICEVOX:<character>" is required; the terms are copied next to the files)
- `voicevox_say`, built from `tools/tts/voicevox_say.c`
