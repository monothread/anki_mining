The bundled dictionary (`jmdict.db`) is generated by `scripts/build-dictionary.sh` and is not stored
in git. It contains JMdict (English meanings), by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development
Group, CC BY-SA 4.0, through jmdict-simplified; the pitch accents of Kanjium (CC BY-SA 4.0); and word
frequencies counted from the Tatoeba Japanese sentences (CC BY 2.0 FR) with `scripts/build-frequency.sh`.
