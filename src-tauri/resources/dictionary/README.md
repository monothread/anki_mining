The bundled dictionary (`jmdict.db`) is generated by `scripts/build-dictionary.sh` and is not stored
in git. It contains:

- JMdict (English meanings), by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, CC BY-SA 4.0,
  through jmdict-simplified.
- Portuguese meanings from the Portuguese Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0 and GFDL), extracted by kaikki.org
  (`data/ptwiktionary-ja.jsonl.gz`).
